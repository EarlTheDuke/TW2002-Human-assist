# planet-nw-construction-v1

Starting a citadel spends the goods at once. The level is not finished until the build day. Net worth drops by those goods for the whole wait, then jumps back. On a class U level 1 that drop is 308,600.

`PLANET_NW_MODE` defaults to `legacy`, so the drop stays and the pin and the scripted games stay put.

`live` scores the goods already spent on the level that is still building, at the same base prices as the stockpile. Extra goods still on the planet still count. A finished level is not counted twice. The growth-dividend tax does not use this number.

Do not turn the switch on until Ben says so.
