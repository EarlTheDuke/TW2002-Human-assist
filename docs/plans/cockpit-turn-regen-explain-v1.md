# cockpit-turn-regen-explain-v1

Turns left can rise while a seat is waiting, because spent turns come back as time passes. The page used to change the number and say nothing.

When the same day shows more turns than the last look, the cockpit says how many came back, that they are spread across 24 hours, and that they stop at the daily cap. A new day still refills without that sentence. No engine switch.
