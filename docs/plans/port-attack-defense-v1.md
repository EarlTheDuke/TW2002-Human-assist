# port-attack-defense-v1

`PORT_ATTACK_MODE` stays `legacy`. Live, an attack with target `port` asks first. The confirmation text is the one from the class 6 attack in REPORT2 section 8. Saying yes costs 5 alignment and the fighters you send.

Class 6 is the only measured port: defensive rating 98, laser barrage 598. That barrage is more than a Merchant Cruiser's 400 shields, so the cruiser is destroyed after the fighters are spent. A class 6 port is not removed. The handbook line that destroying a port costs 50 alignment and pays 50 experience was not seen in that attack (the ship died first), so this slice does not destroy the port.

Other port classes have no measured rating or barrage. A live attack there still costs the 5 alignment and the fighters, and the ship stays. No number was invented for them.
