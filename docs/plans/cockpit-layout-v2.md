# cockpit-layout-v2

On a wide screen the middle column is 60 percent of the row, and the known-space map fills that column. The scene window stays above the map. The left column is still where you are. The right column is still the ship and the port. The buttons stay in the middle column, under the map, because the page tests look for them there.

Drag the map to pan. The wheel and the plus and minus buttons zoom. Re-center, or the c key, puts the whole known map back. A sector you have not visited is still a short stub with no stored coordinate. When more than 36 known sectors are on screen, only the current sector, ports, and the selected sector keep a number. The ring on your sector pulses unless the browser asks for less motion.
