# cash-tax-v1

The daily tax is already the TWGS rule. The MBBS manual says that if you enter with over 100,000 credits on you, you are taxed 5 percent of the total. This copy does that. The tax is the floor of the whole cash pile times 5 over 100. It applies only when alignment is at least 0 and cash is over 100,000. Banked credits are not taxed.

A trader with 105,000 pays 5,250 and is left with 99,750, under the line. That drop is the rule. Taxing only the amount over the line was a planted bug in the bank slice, and it stays wrong.

The Bible says that over 50,000 credits you lose 10 percent of the money you have at that moment. That is the other reading. The bank slice already set it aside. It is not turned on here.

A new trader with 20,000 credits pays nothing. Game 5's shrink was a trader carrying about 200,000 on the ship. Parking the spare cash in the bank still shelters it. A pirate still pays nothing.

No new switch was added. The live formula is already on through the bank mode, and the Bible formula was not chosen.

Do not change the rate until Ben says so.
