# dashboard-player-panel-v1

Clicking a leaderboard row left the follow menu on Moving, and the Details button sat inside the card header where a click did not land. The card also printed the name twice. The drawer said Turns 0/0 and named the sector with no port or planet.

A row click now follows that ship. Details sits on the card and opens the drawer. The name is on the header once. Turns use the day's count. The sector line names the port or planet when one is there. The leaderboard says what the bar and the icons mean.
