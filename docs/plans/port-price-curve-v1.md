# port-price-curve-v1

`PORT_PRICE_MODE` defaults to `legacy`. Today's prices stay put until Ben turns the switch on.

`live` sets a full port to the screen anchors, then keeps today's slope. A full port sells fuel at 16, organics at 35, and equipment at 36. It buys them at 38, 73, and 102. Empty stock still makes a sell dearer and a buy richer. MCIC still moves the quote. Experience still matters, and it stops changing the price at 1000. A federal port stays on the base price.

Fuel 16 and equipment 36 are the full-port sell readings. Organics 35 is the queue's full-port figure. The screen also showed organics at 18.6, so 35 is the high end of that band, not one port. The buy numbers are the middle of the ranges that were seen. Those screens did not say the stock, so calling them a full port is UNVERIFIED.

Do not turn the switch on until Ben says so.
