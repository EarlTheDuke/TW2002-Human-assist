Planetary Trading

From TradeWars Documentation Wiki

Jump to: navigation, search

If you upgrade a planet citadel it becomes like a ship after the Transwarp drive is built. Using Fuel Ore you can move from sector to sector and trade cargo like a ship.

Before a Transwarp drive is built if the planet is in a sector with a port, or you built a port and it finishes, you can trade cargo with the port provided it is buying the cargo. Sell organics and equipment and save fuel ore for colonizing and moving the planet.

The easiest way to trade with a planet is the Planet negotiation option when you dock with a port:


Command [TL=00:00:00]:[12345] (?=Help)? : P

<A> Attack this Port <N> Negotiate Planetary Trade Agreement <T> Trade at this Port <Q> Quit, nevermind

Enter your choice [T] ? N

< 22> Deneb
< 26> Oceanworld
< 27> Olympus

Negotiate agreement with which planet <Q to abort> ?26


We are buying up to 3000. You have 3000 on planet Oceanworld. How many units of Fuel Ore do you want to sell [3000]?0

We'll buy them for 215,987 credits. Your offer [215,987]?215987 Very well, we'll buy them.

The planet negotiation option isn't the best price but it is quick. It is good to use in limited turn games.

Another method is to use a warp drive in your ship to use fuel ore to transwarp to a port and sell the product and then transwarp back to the planet and sell some more. Scripts are good for that to calcuate how much ore is needed.

The planet buy down is simply using a ship with the most cargo holds you can afford to take products off the planet and sell them to the port one trip at a time. Combine this with a planet with Transwarp and you can move the planet and then sell off the products at each port. Even buy down a port using credits to fill the planet up, and then transwarp the planet to another port to sell it off. One can earn millions of credits this way.

An advanced trick is to use the "O" option at the main menu to upgrade the ports to buy and sell more products, once they refresh and regenerate they will buy and sell more. Just keep moving the planet and buying and selling products and don't sell your fuel ore to use as a fuel and you'll do great.

Planet trading is at an advanced stage of the game. It means you made a planet with the T-Warp citidel and can move it like a ship to each sector to sell products to the ports. Don't sell fuel ore as it is powering the T-Warps. Sell equipment and organics.

In the Citadel menu you can use the “P” option to move the planet and like the T-Warp in ships it needs a fighter beacon to T-Warp.

Two ways to planet trade, use the “N” option when you dock your ship at a port to negotiate a trade deal with a planet in the same sector, or you can take products from the planet in your cargo holds and dock your ship with the port and sell them that way. If you have a ship with T-Warp and you find a port that sells fuel ore and buys organics or equipment you can T-Warp from the planet to the port and sell org or equ and buy fuel ore and T-Warp back. Scripts can do this.
