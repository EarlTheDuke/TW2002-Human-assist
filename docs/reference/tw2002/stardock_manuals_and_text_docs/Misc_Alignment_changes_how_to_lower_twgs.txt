> the exp loss is one fourth of the least extreme alignment on the corp if
> it is a mixed corp.
>
> So, Reds w/ -10k,-9k,-5k or so and blues w/ 1k,200,2k:
> 	extreme aligns: -10k, 2k
> 	least extreme: 2k
> 	loss:	2k/4 -> 500 exp
>
> Or, Reds w/ -1k, -4k, -500 and blues w/ 6k,7k,5k
> 	extreme aligns: -1k, 7k
> 	least extreme: -1k
> 	loss:   1k/4 -> 250 exp
>
