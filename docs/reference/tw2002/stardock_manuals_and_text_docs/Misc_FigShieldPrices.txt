price=(base+amp/2) + [ sin(day/period * 2*pi) * amp/2 ]

base=160 (base price - lowest price)
amp=80 (base price + amp = 240 - max price is actually 239)
day=[day of year starting with jan 1]
period=87 (number of days in cycle)

so for today, day=103
price=(160+80/2) + [sin(103/87 * 2*pi) * 80/2] = 163 credits...
