# Cockpit redesign

The current cockpit at `/bot` stays as it is. This page is `/bot2`.

What stays on screen all the time: who you are, the sector, turns left, credits, alignment, experience, and the day. Under that, the view out the window (the same stills and action clips) and the ship you are flying. The ship drawing follows the hull, and the board lists fighters, shields, holds, cargo, mines, genesis torpedoes, probes, photons, the scanner, and the transwarp drive.

Commands sit on one row, one letter each, the way the original prompt did. Warp targets are numbered beside the view. Anything else is behind `?`. A command that needs numbers opens one short form. The engine still decides what is legal.

Colours are the old 16-colour terminal set: black screen, bright cyan frames, yellow prompts, green numbers.
