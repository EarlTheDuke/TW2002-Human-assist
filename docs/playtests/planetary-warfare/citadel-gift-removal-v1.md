# Citadel gift removal

Completing a citadel no longer adds fighters or shields. The gift constants are 0. A planet that already has a garrison keeps it. Credit costs are unchanged.

The siege, hazard, quasar, atmosphere, photon, interdict, transwarp, and transporter lab tables set fighters and shields themselves. They do not complete a citadel, so those tables did not change.
