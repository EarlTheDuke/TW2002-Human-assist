# cockpit-polish-v1

Two figures on the cockpit were sentinels printed as if they were real. A planet whose organics are not falling said they last 9,999 days. An account with no ceiling said the room was 1,000,000,000,000. The first now says the organics are holding. The second says there is no ceiling. The verb heading no longer names the engine list.

The map already marks a one-way warp, and the hold button already shows how many actions are queued. A count of missed turns is not on the page yet.
