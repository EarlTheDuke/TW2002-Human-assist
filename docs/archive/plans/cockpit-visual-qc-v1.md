# cockpit-visual-qc-v1

The cockpit is photographed at 1920 by 1080, 1440 by 900, and 390 by 844. The eight pictures are the lobby, deep space with the map re-centered, docked at a port, landed on a planet, a fighter challenge, the escape pod, a clip playing, and the same page with visuals turned off.

Copies sit in docs/playtests/cockpit-visuals-v1 and in the Commander's cockpit asset screens folder. The engine is unchanged.

The 36 dropped scene pictures are copied in as webp, each under 300 KB, and the scene table points at them. A jpg the Commander drops is converted; the older keys-shaped additions file still works.
