# terra-colonists-form-v1

The Terra form no longer fills in a hold count. Blank is not sent. The preview says how many colonists Terra has, and how much room is left.

One hold is one colonist until the Terra switch is live. Then one hold is 1,000 colonists, and the result names that count. With the switch off, the sentence is the same as before. Colonists already had a cargo row. The cargo tip no longer says empty when they are the only thing aboard.
