# universe-topology-v1

`UNIVERSE_TOPOLOGY_MODE` stays `legacy`.

Off, generation is the graph the recorded games use. Every sector has a way out, and every sector can get back to sector 1.

On, Big Bang lanes are allowed. A one-way can leave no way back. A sector can have no warp out. A few one-way tunnels join distant sectors: one per 250 sectors, and at least one on a map of 80 or more. The handbook does not name that count. One pair of sectors is closed so a ship can enter and cannot leave. FedSpace stays open. Sector 1 can still reach every sector. A plotted course from a dead end says there is no route. Ferrengal prefers a real dead end when one sits at least 8 hops out. The map hint stops saying the universe is fully connected.

The live profile stays off. This flag is added to that profile at the Batch M gate.
