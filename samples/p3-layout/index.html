B — Readout rail

Same 1.5fr / 1fr deck as A. Inside the ship cell, the stat block and the holds move to a rail beside the picture so the stage gets the full row height. The deck bands do not change. The stacked order under the picture does.

C — Center span

Three tracks. The sector report takes a left rail, the ship spans both rows in the middle, and the map stays on the right with COMMS under it. The hull is geometrically central. The third track spends width the two-track grid gives to the ship and the map.
